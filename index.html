My Journey

A lifelong creative exploring media as diverse as sound sculpture, fibre art, painting, poetry, and soap making, my practice currently exists at the intersection of photography, digital manipulation, and blockchain-based community art. After three decades as a professional chef, a career-ending injury pushed me into software development—a shift that fundamentally changed my creative practice and kick-started an incredible new artistic odyssey.

I discovered that debugging code, like recipe development, is about transformation: taking ideas and raw materials and reshaping them into something new that provides a nourishing experience. Software development offered something my earlier media couldn't—the ability to create work that scales, persists, and invites participation. Code lets me turn ideas into interactive experiences. Now I work creatively in this digital realm, treating technology as both medium and collaborator in building more inclusive digital spaces.

See what I'm cooking up

What excites me is that this technology is a studio, a megaphone, and a toolkit all in one. It amplifies creativity, connects communities, and opens sustainable paths for artists who've been shut out of traditional systems. I'm building tools and experiences that let marginalized creators own their work, reach audiences directly, and thrive on their own terms—using code and blockchain as levers for real equity.

As an artist and developer committed to social justice, I see technology as a way to dismantle gatekeeping and redistribute power. Our world maintains systemic barriers that keep too many people from accessing resources and opportunities. My work challenges that.

What that looks like in practice:

  • Creative Advocacy: Art and tech projects that amplify marginalized voices and challenge inequity.
  • Inclusive Design: Tools and platforms built for accessibility, ensuring people of all abilities and backgrounds can participate.
  • Alternative Economics: Community-driven platforms and revenue models that empower creators and redistribute resources.
  • Civic Technology: Projects that advocate for environmental justice, equitable access, and inclusive public spaces.
  • Community Empowerment: Collaborative spaces where diverse groups co-create solutions for a more just future.

Mission

My mission is to build tools, platforms, and creative experiences that dismantle barriers, redistribute power, and enable marginalized artists and communities to own their work, reach global audiences, and thrive. By working together and wielding technology as our ally, we can create the systemic changes that truly set us all free.

Not Just Covid

Clean Air Advocacy

Organizations and initiatives focused on promoting mandated collective indoor clean air environments. Our goal is to help everyone access their right to clean air in their homes, schools, and workplaces.

News and Science Updates

Stay informed about the latest research and developments in clean air advocacy. News and articles about COVID-19, Disability Justice, Long covid, clean air legislation, and more.

Games and Infographics

Engaging games and infographics designed to raise awareness about clean air issues and promote advocacy efforts and help get you connected to the resources you need to have your voice heard in the fight for clean air justice.

Disability Justice

Being able-bodied is a temporary state. We must work together to create a more inclusive world for people of all abilities. As more of us become disabled by long COVID and other chronic illnesses, it's crucial to advocate for accessibility and support. Clean air is a basic human right that affects everyone, regardless of ability.

ORDINAL RAINBOWS

ART ON CHAIN

Wild-harvested, handcrafted and ethically sourced, Non-AI art. ORDINAL RAINBOWS is an NFT collection that fuses digital artistry with a deep fascination and reverence for rainbows as the fleeting and elusive optical illusions they are. Founded by artist ChefMyKLove, it's a project about hunting and harvesting these phenomena from everywhere all around us, using novel, innovative techniques, of his own design. This creates unique digital artifacts that can be inscribed onto the blockchain, eventually in some unique and innovative ways

This project depicts the fleeting illusion of rainbows and of light refractions in general. The Rainbow hunting and harvesting techniques use an ethical, all-natural process, eschewing AI generation for hands-on light bending and artist-led digital manipulation. This creates the raw materials for inscription-grade digital art compiled into an engaging NFT series and minted onto individual Satoshis on Bitcoin's most authentic and scaleable protocol, BSV.

Infused with the vibrant inclusivity of the artist's queer identity, The ORDINAL RAINBOWS palette celebrates diversity while exploring rainbows as metaphors for hidden truths in ALL of us. The Drop details are still unclear, though we aim to have the Season 1 OG mint ready for launch by end of q2, 2026... We hope to get the game launched by the end of 2026

Stay Updated

Season 1 mint is only the beginning. By 2027, The ORDINAL RAINBOWS platform will be a full fledged 1sat Ordinals minting and trading platform, where users can mint their own collections or fungible tokens, Achieve ORDINAL RAINBOWS game-generated artifacts, and trade any manner of collections on Bitcoin.
