My Journey

A lifelong creative person, exploring media as diverse as sound sculpture, fibre art, painting, poetry and soap making, my practice currently exists at the intersection of photography, digital image manipulation, software and blockchain development, and community-engaged art-making. After three decades as a professional chef, a career-ending injury pushed me into software development—a shift that fundamentally changed how I think about creation itself—kick-starting an incredible new creative odyssey.

I discovered that debugging code, like recipe development, is about transformation: taking ideas and raw materials and reshaping them into something new that provides a nourishing experience. Also that software development and technology are a medium for making things that scale, persist, and include. Code lets me turn ideas into interactive experiences. Now I work my creativity with digital illustration, interactive narrative design, and multi-faceted transmedia ecosystems, treating technology as both medium and collaborator in building more equitable creative ecosystems.

What I see is that this tech is a studio, a megaphone, and a toolkit all in one! It amplifies creativity, helps to turn activism into practical systems, and opens sustainable paths for artists and communities to connect and thrive. The possibilities to further dissolve boundaries between us by using technology are endless...

And I'm on Board for THAT!!

As an artist, activist, and techno-futurist uncovering our collective potential to drive innovation and create a more equitable, inclusive future, I leverage my passions and skills to advocate for transformative social justice and radical change. Our deeply unjust world is riddled with systematic inequities that keep so many people marginalized, cut off from vital resources and opportunities. My mission is to find ways to help empower all of us harness our unique privileges, no matter our individual identities, to collectively drive the changes that will truly set us all free.

What “all that” looks like in practice:

  • Creative Advocacy: Develop art and tech projects, like interactive installations or apps, that amplify marginalized voices and challenge systemic inequities.
  • Inclusive Design: Create tools and platforms prioritizing accessibility, ensuring people of all abilities and backgrounds can engage and contribute.
  • Equitable Economic Systems: Build alternative revenue models, such as community-driven NFT platforms or micropayment systems, to empower creators and redistribute resources.
  • Civic Technology: Use technology to advocate for public goods, such as environmental justice, equitable access to resources, and inclusive public spaces.
  • Community Empowerment: Foster collaborative spaces where diverse groups can harness their unique strengths to co-create solutions for a more just future.

Mission

I build tools, platforms, and creative experiences that dismantle gatekeeping, redistribute power and resources, and enable marginalized artists and communities to own their work, reach global audiences, and thrive—using code and blockchain as levers for real-world equity and collective liberation.

Not Just Covid

Clean Air Advocacy

Organizations and initiatives focused on promoting mandated collective indoor clean air environments. Our goal is to help everyone access their right to clean air in their homes, schools, and workplaces.

News and Science Updates

Stay informed about the latest research and developments in clean air advocacy. News and articles about COVID-19, Disability Justice, Long covid, clean air legislation, and more.

Games and Infographics

Engaging games and infographics designed to raise awareness about clean air issues and promote advocacy efforts and help get you connected to the resources you need to have your voice heard in the fight for clean air justice.

Disability Justice

Being able-bodied is a temporary state. We must work together to create a more inclusive world for people of all abilities. As more of us become disabled by long COVID and other chronic illnesses, it's crucial to advocate for accessibility and support. Clean air is a basic human right that affects everyone, regardless of ability.

ORDINAL RAINBOWS

ART ON CHAIN

Wild-harvested, handcrafted and ethically sourced, Non-AI art. ORDINAL RAINBOWS is an NFT collection that fuses digital artistry with a deep fascination and reverence for rainbows as the fleeting and elusive optical illusions they are. Founded by artist ChefMyKLove, it's a project about hunting and harvesting these phenomena from everywhere all around us, using novel, innovative techniques, of his own design. This creates unique digital artifacts that can be inscribed onto the blockchain, eventually in some unique and innovative ways

This project depicts the fleeting illusion of rainbows and of light refractions in general. The Rainbow hunting and harvesting techniques use an ethical, all-natural process, eschewing AI generation for hands-on light bending and artist-led digital manipulation. This creates the raw materials for inscription-grade digital art compiled into an engaging NFT series and minted onto individual Satoshis on Bitcoin's most authentic and scaleable protocol, BSV.

Infused with the vibrant inclusivity of the artist's queer identity, The ORDINAL RAINBOWS palette celebrates diversity while exploring rainbows as metaphors for hidden truths in ALL of us. The Drop details are still unclear, though we aim to have the Season 1 OG mint ready for launch by end of q2, 2026... We hope to get the game launched by the end of 2026

Stay Updated

Season 1 mint is only the beginning. By 2027, The ORDINAL RAINBOWS platform will be a full fledged 1sat Ordinals minting and trading platform, where users can mint their own collections or fungible tokens, Achieve ORDINAL RAINBOWS game-generated artifacts, and trade any manner of collections on Bitcoin.
